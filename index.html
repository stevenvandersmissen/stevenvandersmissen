[ ABOUT ]

forever student, full-time teacher.

19 years at TSM Mechelen, and still learning something new every week.

I've been teaching at TSM Mechelen for 19 years, covering web design, 2D and interactive animation, UI/UX, 3D design, game development, coding, image editing, digital graphic design and AI.

Outside of teaching I like to work on small graphic design and programming projects for family, friends and colleagues. I see myself as a “forever student”, always exploring new skills through tutorials, online courses and personal projects.
